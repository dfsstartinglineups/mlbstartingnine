Welcome to MLB9 Lineup Alerts 🔔

Never take a zero again. Get instant push notifications when your players are scratched.

  1. Build Your Watchlist: Search for specific players using the search bar, or drag and drop your active DraftKings or FanDuel entries CSV file. We will automatically extract your entire player pool and map them to our live database so you never take a zero.

    1. Log into FanDuel.com on a desktop or laptop.
    2. Navigate to the Upcoming tab to view your entries.
    3. Click the CSV Edit button located on the right side of the page.
    4. Select your slate and click Download CSV template.
    5. Drag and drop that downloaded file into our tool below!

    1. Log into DraftKings.com on a desktop or laptop.
    2. Click on the Lineups tab in the top navigation menu.
    3. Click the Edit Entries button near the top right.
    4. Filter for your specific Sport and Game Set (slate).
    5. Click Download to save your `.csv` file and drag it into our tool below!
  2. Choose Your Alert Level:
    • Scratches Only: We will only bother you if disaster strikes. You get a push notification the exact second one of your players is officially left out of the starting nine.
    • Full Coverage: Get a complete roll-call. We will ping you when your players are confirmed in the lineup, and alert you if they are scratched or removed late.
  3. Arm Your Alerts: To ensure MLB starting lineups reach your screen instantly, you must grant push notification permissions. The process varies slightly by device:
    • 🍎 iPhone & iPad (iOS 16.4+): You must add this page to your home screen. Tap the Share icon (square with an up arrow)—located at the bottom center in Safari, or the top right address bar in Chrome. Scroll down and select "Add to Home Screen". Open the new MLB9 icon from your home screen, then tap "Confirm & Arm Alerts".
    • 💻 Mac (Safari): Click "Confirm & Arm Alerts" and select "Allow" when the Safari permission prompt drops down from the top of the window.
    • 🖥️ Desktop (Chrome, Edge, Firefox): Click the arm button and select "Allow" on the browser popup near the address bar. (Note: Keep your browser open in the background to receive live alerts.)
    • 📱 Android (Chrome): Tap the arm button and select "Allow" on the permission prompt that appears on your screen.
