DFS Lineup Checker ⚾

Instantly cross-reference your DraftKings or FanDuel CSV against official MLB starting lineups.

FanDuel
  1. Log into FanDuel.com on a desktop/laptop.
  2. Navigate to the Upcoming tab.
  3. Click CSV Edit on the right side.
  4. Select your slate and click Download CSV template.
DraftKings
  1. Log into DraftKings.com on a desktop/laptop.
  2. Click Lineups in the top menu.
  3. Click Edit Entries near the top right.
  4. Click Download to save your `.csv` file.
